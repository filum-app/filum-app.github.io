Four things to know before you go in

Do not upload any real data

Everything entered is visible to other visitors, and erased at the next reset. For a medical device design file, both are serious. The file on display is entirely fictional — product, people, values, suppliers. One exception, and it is visible: the administrator account bears the publisher's name, so that is the name that will be printed on whatever you sign there.

This is not a qualified installation

Nothing signed there has any value: everyone signs in under everyone's name. A real installation is single-organisation, behind your infrastructure, with your accounts and your passwords.

Everything is reset regularly

The instance returns to its original state at a fixed interval. That is also what repairs it: if a visitor has changed a password, the next reset reopens the door. An account locked by failed attempts reopens on its own after a few minutes — the lock is time-based, not permanent. The application displays the reset period on every screen.

The assistant is deliberately switched off

Filum runs on YOUR language-model key, never ours. On an instance with published credentials, a key placed there would be an open bill to whoever finds it. The assistant is therefore switched off in the settings and has no key — both, not one or the other: removing the key while leaving the switch on would have made the screen say the opposite of this sentence. The seed check refuses to produce a demonstration where it is enabled.

Where to start

Open a released document and look at its signatures: each carries the fingerprint of the content it attests, and that fingerprint survives the next revision. Then open the audit trail: the chain is verified on screen, entry by entry. Then, signed in as KGA — deletion is reserved to the administrator —, try to delete a released document: the refusal is explicit and points you to the compliant route, a new revision.

Finally, one gesture you can perform yourself: sign in as SBA and open PLN-3010. It is distributed to you and you have not read it yet — acknowledge receipt. The list goes from two to three, your name is frozen into the record, and the audit trail logs it. Then release a revision of the document: your reading will not follow it.
