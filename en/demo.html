Four things to know before you go in

Do not upload any real data

Everything entered is visible to other visitors, and erased at the next reset. For a medical device design file, both are serious. The file on display is entirely fictional — product, people, values, suppliers.

This is not a qualified installation

Nothing signed there has any value: everyone signs in under everyone's name. A real installation is single-organisation, behind your infrastructure, with your accounts and your passwords.

Everything is reset regularly

The instance returns to its original state at a fixed interval. That is also what repairs it: if a visitor has changed a password or locked an account, the next reset reopens the door. The application displays the period on every screen.

The assistant is deliberately switched off

Filum runs on YOUR language-model key, never ours. On an instance with published credentials, a key placed there would be an open bill to whoever finds it: the assistant therefore reports itself as “not configured”, as on a fresh installation.

Where to start

Open a released document and look at its signatures: each carries the fingerprint of the content it attests, and that fingerprint survives the next revision. Then open the audit trail: the chain is verified on screen, entry by entry. Finally, try to delete a released document — the refusal is explicit and points you to the compliant route, a new revision. That is the heart of the product, and it takes three gestures.
